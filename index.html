About the Clinic

About Shree Sant Gajanan Pharmaclinic

Shree Sant Gajanan Pharmaclinic is a healthcare clinic and pharmacy in Wanadongri, Nagpur, focused on personalized consultation and holistic wellness. The clinic provides consultation across Ayurveda, Homeopathy, Naturopathy and Hypnotherapy, with attention to each patient’s individual concerns, history and needs.
